## Abstract
Stochastic gradient descent (SGD) is at the center of modern machine learning and is known to be statistically minimax-optimal in many regression settings, e.g., for convex Generalized Linear Models (GLMs). Yet, it is unknown whether its simplest differentially private Noisy SGD counterpart fulfills similar favorable guarantees even on strongly convex losses. We resolve this question affirmatively. We upper bound the parameter estimation error for a single pass of Noisy SGD with a fixed stepsize schedule and constant batch sizes on smooth and strongly convex Lipschitz losses over a compact convex domain under Renyi DP. Specialized to strongly convex GLMs, our error bounds match minimax lower bounds we derive based on the Score Attack framework. In particular, this provides the first minimax-optimal rate for Noisy SGD in private logistic regression, avoiding poly-logarithmic terms present in existing results. Our analysis is versatile as it relies on standard convex analysis and establishes the same rates in expectation and with high probability.
